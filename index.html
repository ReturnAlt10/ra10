WhatsApp Chat Exporter

Turn your WhatsApp chat exports into clean, printable chat views or structured tables. Works entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

01

Export from WhatsApp

iOS: Open chat → tap name → scroll down → Export Chat → Without Media.

Android: Open chat → ⋮ → More → Export Chat → Without Media.

02

Upload the file

Drag the .txt file into the tool below, or click Browse. The file is processed entirely in your browser.

03

Export your way

Switch between Chat and Table view, filter by date or search messages, then hit Print or Download PDF.
