## What is already done well

- **Privilege escalation on `profiles` is blocked by a trigger.** Credits can only go down from the client, and tier and unlimited credits cannot change.
- **Owner checks use the JWT email, never `profiles.email`.**
- **Stripe webhooks are signature-verified;** checkout has a `PAYMENTS_ENABLED` kill switch.
- **XP goes through a single, guarded `security definer` RPC** with rate limits and activity requirements.
- **Legal-terms acceptance is enforced server-side** (`_shared/legal.ts`) for AI features.
- **Forums have AI moderation with a human-review log** and restore.
- **Copyrighted sources are git-ignored** for every pipeline built since 2026 (only the 11 legacy PDFs remain, R-03).
