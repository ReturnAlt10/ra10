# Example Questions and Mark Schemes for Training

## 1-2 Mark Questions (Give/State/Identify)

Q: Give two functions of money. (2)
MS: Award 1 mark each up to 2: Unit of account / Store of value / Means of exchange / Basis of credit / Legal tender

Q: Identify two features of a standing order. (2)
MS: Fixed amounts (1) / Consumer authorises it (1) / Set at regular intervals (1) / Consumer sets the date and amount (1) / Can be cancelled by consumer (1)

Q: State two types of revenue expenditure. (2)
MS: Wages (1) / Rent (1) / Utility bills (1) / Advertising (1) / Insurance (1) — accept any appropriate

Q: Give two labels used on a break-even chart. (2)
MS: Break-even point / Fixed costs / Variable costs / Total costs / Total revenue / Area of profit / Area of loss / Margin of safety / Quantity / Break-even quantity — accept any two

## 2-4 Mark Questions (Explain)

Q: Explain one advantage of using hire purchase to buy equipment. (2)
MS: Do not need to pay full amount upfront (1) which helps improve the business cash flow (1)
OR: Asset is owned at end of HP period (1) therefore the business has an asset it can sell (1)
OR: Can access more expensive assets (1) as cost is spread over time (1)

Q: Explain two advantages to a consumer of using mobile banking. (4)
MS: Can access account at any time (1) so do not need to travel to a branch which saves time (1)
Can make payments quickly (1) which means bills can be paid on time to avoid charges (1)
Can check balance instantly (1) helping the consumer manage their finances and avoid going overdrawn (1)

Q: Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of using a payday loan. (4)
MS: Advantage: Quick to access (1) useful for short-term cash flow problems (1)
Disadvantage: Very high APR (1) meaning the consumer pays back much more than borrowed (1)

## 6 Mark Discuss Question

Q: Morgan is 23 years old with a £2,000 credit card balance at 19.9% APR and 
£1,000 car loan at 7% APR with 12 months remaining. He has been offered a new 
credit card at 0% for 12 months rising to 29.9% after. Discuss whether Morgan 
should transfer his debts to the new card. (6)

MS (Levels-based):
L1 1-2: Basic description of 0% offer or generic points about debt
L2 3-4: Some weighing up e.g. saves interest in short term but risk of high rate after
L3 5-6: Well-developed balanced discussion e.g. saving on both debts in short term, 
must clear balance within 12 months otherwise 29.9% is higher than current rates, 
suitable if Morgan is disciplined, inappropriate if he may not clear the balance

Indicative content:
- Saves interest on £2,000 at 19.9% during 12 months
- Car loan ends in 12 months anyway so minimal benefit transferring it
- 29.9% after 12 months is higher than both current rates — risk if not cleared
- Transfer fees may apply
- Good option if Morgan has discipline to clear balance within 12 months
- Not suitable if Morgan struggles to manage debt

## 12 Mark Evaluate Question

Q: Takudzwa is planning a European camper van trip with his family. Each family 
member has a kayak worth £400 and phone worth £300. He has researched four 
insurance providers [table with cover details, prices, limits]. Evaluate which 
insurance product would be best for Takudzwa. (12)

MS (Levels-based L1-L4):
Indicative content should consider:
- Cost vs coverage balance
- Whether cancellation cover is needed
- Medical cover adequacy for Europe
- Item values vs claim limits (4 kayaks = £1600, phones = £1200)
- Cash limits relevance
- Conclusion must be justified and linked to Takudzwa's specific circumstances

## Calculation Questions

Q: Silva's fixed costs are £5,100. Each plant sells for £14. Variable costs are 
£5.50 for the plant and £2.50 for pot and soil. Calculate the break-even point. (4)
MS: Contribution per unit = £14 - £5.50 - £2.50 = £6 (1)
Break-even = £5,100 / £6 = 850 units (1) workings shown (2)
