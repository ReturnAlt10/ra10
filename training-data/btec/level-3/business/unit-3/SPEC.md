## Key Formulas
Break-even = Fixed costs / (Selling price - Variable cost per unit)
Margin of safety = Actual output - Break-even output
Contribution per unit = Selling price - Variable cost per unit
Total contribution = Contribution per unit x Units sold
Gross profit margin = (Gross profit / Revenue) x 100
Profit margin = (Profit for year / Revenue) x 100
Mark-up = (Gross profit / Cost of sales) x 100
Current ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities
Acid test = (Current assets - Inventories) / Current liabilities
ROCE = (Profit for year / Capital employed) x 100
Straight line depreciation = (Cost - Residual value) / Useful life
Reducing balance depreciation = NBV x depreciation rate %

## Cash Flow Forecast Structure
Rows: Opening balance, Inflows (credit sales, cash sales, other), Total inflows,
Outflows (purchases, wages, rent, advertising, other), Total outflows,
Net cash flow (total inflows - total outflows),
Closing balance (opening balance + net cash flow)

## Income Statement Structure
Revenue
- Cost of sales
= Gross profit
+ Other income (commission received etc)
= Total income
- Expenses (wages, rent, utilities, depreciation etc)
  Note: add accruals to expenses, subtract prepayments
= Profit for the year

## Statement of Financial Position Structure
Non-current assets (cost - accumulated depreciation = NBV)
Current assets (inventories, trade receivables, cash)
Total assets
Current liabilities (trade payables, bank overdraft, accruals)
Non-current liabilities (bank loans)
Net assets
Equity (share capital + retained earnings)
