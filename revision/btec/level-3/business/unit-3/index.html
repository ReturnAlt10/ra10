Q1–Q2 Short-answer boot

Early questions typically test knowledge and basic application: state a definition, identify a formula, calculate a ratio or simple break-even.

Q3 Long scenario task

The major 8–12 mark question usually involves a realistic business scenario where you apply multiple concepts: justify a finance choice, prepare a forecast, analyze ratios.

E1 & F3 Most reusable revision

If time is tight, prioritise E1 (break-even) and F3 (ratios). They recur in both short calculation items and long justify/discuss questions.
