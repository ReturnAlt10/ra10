Q11 — Explain (4 marks)
  • Penetration pricing sets a low initial price to attract customers and gain market share quickly (1) suitable for mass markets with elastic demand (1)
  • Price skimming sets a high initial price to maximise profit from early adopters (1) before lowering it as competition increases (1)
Q12 — Explain (4 marks)
  • Measure against the campaign's aims and objectives (1) e.g. sales, awareness or market share changes (1)
  • Use metrics such as sales data, web traffic, engagement and return on investment (1) to judge effectiveness and inform future campaigns (1)
