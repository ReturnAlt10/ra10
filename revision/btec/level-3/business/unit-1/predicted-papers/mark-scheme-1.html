Q14 — Explain (4 marks)
  • Perfect competition has many small firms selling identical products with no barriers to entry (1); firms are price takers (1)
  • Imperfect competition (e.g. monopoly, oligopoly) has fewer firms, differentiated products and barriers to entry (1); firms have some control over price (1)
