# Learning Aim F Mindmap: Issues

## F1: Moral and Ethical Issues
- **Privacy**: Data use, consent
- **Environmental Impact**: E-waste, energy use
- **Unequal Access**: Digital divide, accessibility
- **Assistive Technology**: Inclusion
- **Online Behaviour**: Netiquette, acceptable use

## F2: Legal Issues
- **Legislation**: Computer misuse, copyright, patents, health & safety, data protection
- **Implications**: Protecting systems, users, data from misuse/attack
