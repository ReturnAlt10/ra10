# Learning Aim D Mindmap: Protecting Data and Information

## D1: Threats to Data
- **External**: Viruses/malware, hackers, accidental damage, social engineering, natural disasters
- **Internal**: Inappropriate access, accidental disclosure, data theft/leak, portable device misuse
- **Impacts**: Data loss, financial/legal loss, reputation damage

## D2: Protecting Data
- **Techniques**: File permissions, access levels, backup/recovery, passwords/MFA, biometrics, physical controls, digital certificates
- **Antivirus**: Features/functions
- **Firewalls**: Features/functions
- **Encryption**: Stored data, transmission, HTTPS

---
### Exam Tips
- List threats and give examples
- Explain protection methods
- Discuss impact of breaches
- Link to real-world cases
