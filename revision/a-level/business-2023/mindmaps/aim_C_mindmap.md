# Learning Aim C Mindmap: Operating Online

## C1: Online Systems
- **Cloud Computing Models**: Private, public, hybrid
  - *IaaS, SaaS, PaaS*: Infrastructure, software, platform as a service
- **Impact/Implications**: Cost, security, scalability, features, ease of use, connectivity
- **Remote Working**: VPNs, remote desktop

## C2: Online Communities
- **Types**: Social media, blogs/vlogs, wikis, chatrooms, IM, podcasts, forums
- **Implications**: User experience, needs, cost, privacy, security, downtime, training, integration, productivity, working practices

---
### Exam Tips
- Compare cloud models
- Discuss pros/cons for individuals vs organisations
- Give examples of online communities
- Explain security/privacy issues
