Know exactly what each paper looks like

All three papers are 2 hours, 100 marks and assess the whole specification (3.1-3.10). What changes is the question style. Built from every AQA 7132 paper, mark scheme and examiner report from 2020 to 2025.

How the levels actually work

Every extended question is marked with a level grid. Examiners read the whole answer, find the best-fit level, then place it within the level. These are the grids AQA uses for each tariff:

16P2 last part, P3 Q3-Q4L1 1-4 · L2 5-8 · L3 9-12 · L4 13-162/3/4/7Balanced, well-developed arguments + a judgement that weighs them.

24P3 Q6L1 1-5 · L2 6-10 · L3 11-15 · L4 16-20 · L5 21-245/4/6/9Contrasting contexts for 'all businesses' and a judgement that flows from the arguments.
25P1 Sections C & DL1 1-5 · L2 6-10 · L3 11-15 · L4 16-20 · L5 21-255/4/6/10Precise knowledge, developed two-sided analysis with examples, weighed judgement.

AO1 knowledge · AO2 application · AO3 analysis · AO4 evaluation. Calculations (2-4 marks) are point-marked with the Own Figure Rule: a correct formula alone usually earns 1 mark, and the final mark needs the right unit.

What the examiners keep saying (reports 2023-2025)

  1. Answer the precise question. 'Will sales increase?' is not 'how should Bodhi increase sales?'. Drift caps the level.
  2. Explain the 'why' in every link. 'Organic growth is less risky' is a statement; why it is less risky (no culture clash, fewer layers) makes it analysis.
  3. Know the model, not just the words. Lewin (driving vs restraining - what has changed now), Herzberg (training is a motivator, not hygiene), stakeholder mapping terms.
  4. Don't confuse look-alike concepts: capacity vs capacity utilisation, differentiation vs diversification, QA vs QC, monetary vs fiscal policy, gross profit vs gross profit margin.
  5. Use the data - then question it. Calculate ratios when they help; note small or old samples (27 families in 2021!).
  6. Conclusions weigh up, they don't summarise. Say which argument matters more and what it depends on. No new points in the conclusion.
  7. 'All businesses' needs contrasting contexts (e.g. fast-moving tech vs slow-moving furniture), not just the case company.
  8. Units matter in calculations. Margin of safety in units, not £; NPV in £m; payables in days.
  9. Analyse questions need no conclusion - a judgement earns nothing extra and costs time.
  10. Time: about a mark a minute. Section D and P3 Q6 are where students run out of time.

What the trend says

  • Strategic methods (3.9) is the biggest share of Section B-D marks: growth, globalisation, innovation and digital technology come up every year.
  • Finance (3.5) is only about 5% of extended marks - just two finance essays in six years (2022 finance function, 2024 ratios for non-profits). A finance essay is overdue.
  • Section B calculations rotate: ratios (2022, 2023, 2025), break-even/variance (2020, 2024), network/decision tree (2021), NPV (2024). Payback and ARR have never been a Section B calculation.
  • Herzberg has never had his own Paper 1 extended question (Maslow 2023, financial v non-financial 2021, soft HRM 2024).
  • Spec v2.0 removed Blake Mouton, Hackman & Oldham, Greiner, Hofstede, Bartlett & Ghoshal, Bowman and the Balanced Scorecard - 9 MCQs and one essay since 2020 used them. Tannenbaum Schmidt is new and has never been examined.

Most likely for June 2027

  • HighA finance essay - cash flow v profit, sources of finance or ratios (3.5)
  • HighHerzberg / does pay motivate? (3.6.4)
  • HighTannenbaum Schmidt leadership continuum - new in spec v2.0 (3.2.1)
  • HighPayback or ARR as a Section B calculation (3.7.8)
  • MediumHR calculations: labour productivity, turnover or labour cost per unit (3.6.2)
  • MediumPorter's five forces or generic strategies essay (3.7.7 / 3.8.2)
  • MediumInflation or taxation 9-marker (3.7.5)
  • MediumBig data, data mining or automation (3.9.4)

What the trend says

  • Human resources has been the focus of just one Paper 3 question in six years (2025 Q4). It is the most under-examined area relative to the specification.
  • Q3 has needed calculations every year: ratios (2022), efficiency data (2023), investment appraisal (2021 Q5, 2024 Q3). Expect payback/ARR/NPV, ratios or break-even.
  • Q5 is a 20-mark decision: two-option choices in 2021 (sites) and 2024 (organic v franchise); financing/risk in 2023; stakeholders in 2022; legislation in 2025.
  • Q6 themes so far: CSR, re-shoring, strategic planning, leadership, innovation, capacity. Not yet: globalisation as opportunity v threat, five forces, technology, competitive advantage of small v large firms.
  • Cases swung from large plcs (2020-21) to small Ltds (2022-24) and back to a large manufacturer (2025) - prepare for both, and for reading balance sheets.
