# AQA A-Level Business Paper 1 Revision Studio

A professional local web app for last-day revision.

## Included

- Full Paper 1 revision guide aligned to topics 3.1 to 3.5.
- High-value formula bank with quick drills.
- Paper trend view and recurring formula map.
- Practice modes: MCQ, fill-in, exam-style, Duolingo-style micro-lessons.
- RA10 AI Examiner marking (same account, credit, and model flow as the main RA10 revision units).
- Flashcards with confidence tagging.
- Score tracking and accuracy.
- Print current view and download revision pack as JSON.

## How to run

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Use the left panel to move across revision sections.
3. Sign in with your RA10 account to use AI Examiner and synced credits.
