Question 1 · Crestwell Coffee Ltd

01.1  [4 marks]  AO1 = 1, AO2 = 3 · 3.5.2

Using Appendix A, calculate the change in Crestwell Coffee Ltd's gross profit margin between 2023 and 2024. Show your working.

Answer

A fall of 3.2 percentage points (from 60.9% to 57.7%)

Marking steps
  • Gross profit 2023 = £18.4m - £7.2m = £11.2m and 2024 = £19.6m - £8.3m = £11.3m (1 mark)
  • Gross profit margin 2023 = 11.2 / 18.4 x 100 = 60.9% (1 mark)
  • Gross profit margin 2024 = 11.3 / 19.6 x 100 = 57.7% (1 mark)
  • Change = a fall of 3.2 percentage points (1 mark)
Additional guidance

Accept -3.2% or 'fell by 3.2%'. A percentage change in the margin (-5.3%) is also credited if clearly labelled. Using operating profit instead (10.3% to 7.7%) = 1 mark for the method of calculating a margin. OFR applies.

02.1  [9 marks]  AO1 = 2, AO2 = 3, AO3 = 4 · 3.6.2

Analyse the possible causes of the trends in Northgate Logistics plc's workforce data shown in Appendix A.

Demands of the question
  • interpret the trends (labour turnover rising from 20% to 28%, falling productivity, rising absence and errors)
  • analyse possible causes, using the context
Indicative content
  • labour turnover has risen from 20% (480/2 400) to 28% (700/2 500); new staff are less experienced, reducing productivity and increasing errors
  • low pay close to the minimum wage - only 42% feel valued - fits Herzberg's hygiene factors: dissatisfaction leads to absence and quitting
  • only 39% feel adequately trained, which may explain the rising error rate and falling orders per worker
  • rising demand may have increased work pressure, raising absence (6.2%)
  • a hard HRM approach (flexible contracts) may reduce commitment
  • the trends are linked: high turnover -> inexperienced staff -> lower productivity -> more pressure -> more leavers

  • Calculating labour turnover is not required but helps develop the analysis. One well-developed cause applied to the data can reach Level 3.
