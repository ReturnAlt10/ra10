Calculate the profit variance and state whether it is adverse or favourable. Show your working.

Answer

£35 000 adverse

Marking steps
  • Actual revenue = £420 000 x 0.95 = £399 000 (adverse variance £21 000) (1 mark)
  • Actual costs = £350 000 x 1.04 = £364 000 (adverse variance £14 000) (1 mark)
  • Budgeted profit £70 000; actual profit £35 000 (1 mark)
  • Profit variance = £35 000 adverse (1 mark - 'adverse' must be stated)
Additional guidance

Correct figure without stating 'adverse' = 3 marks. OFR applies.

17  [4 marks]  AO1 = 1, AO2 = 3 · 3.7.2

Calculate the company's inventory turnover. Show your working.

Answer

6 times a year

Marking steps
  • Average inventory = (£1.4m + £1.8m) / 2 = £1.6m (2 marks)
  • Inventory turnover = £9.6m / £1.6m (1 mark)
  • = 6 times (1 mark)
Additional guidance

Using closing inventory only (5.3 times) = 3 marks. Formula alone = 1 mark.
